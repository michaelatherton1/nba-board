Track record since launch

Team ratings

  • Model: opponent-adjusted pace and efficiency ratings plus a correction layer for rest, back-to-backs, altitude and form. Walk-forward backtest 2016-17 to 2025-26: average miss 14.7 points vs the market line's 14.4.
  • Model v2 (Nov 2026): better team ratings (shooting luck discounted), a home-arena effect, and player availability. Morning-of version by default; when an injury report is available it uses the report. Walk-forward 2025-26: average miss 14.74 points (old model 14.85, closing line 14.51).
  • No edge vs the closing line: against true closing totals the model has been about 50% in every season tested. Against opening totals its 6+ point gaps hit 58-66% from 2016-17 to 2024-25 (lines then moved toward it), but 2025-26 went 61-62, so the opening-line rule is a small-stakes forward test, judged by line movement.
  • No injury data. A big gap often means the market knows about an injury the model doesn't.
  • Prices are taker asks for one $1 contract, with each venue's taker fee added (Kalshi 0.07·p·(1−p) × series multiplier; Polymarket US its fee coefficient · p·(1−p); Novig charges nothing before tip-off). Odds shown are American odds equivalent after fees. Lines move fast, so check the venue before trading.
  • Arbitrage pairs buy both sides of the same proposition on different venues. Whole-number lines are left out (venues settle pushes differently). Read both rulebooks before trading; edges over 5¢ are usually a rules mismatch or a stale quote.
  • Data: ESPN via sportsdataverse, Kalshi, Polymarket US and Novig public market data. Not financial advice.
